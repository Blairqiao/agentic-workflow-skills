# Feature taskboard

Track, current phase, update date, and plan link:

## Workspace and starting state

Working directory, branch or base revision, pre-existing user changes, baseline check status, and isolated resource allocations if any.

## Tasks

Keep unfinished work easy to find. Retain small completed tasks as compact checkboxes; when the board grows large, move detailed completed task notes to `.agent-docs/archive/` and keep a short summary here.

| Task | Status | Red and green evidence | Blockers or next action |

## Decisions and attempted approaches

Record material implementation adjustments and brief notes on any failed attempts. When an in-place run hits the three-attempt cap and rolls back candidate files, record what was tried and why it failed here.

## Review

Review method, independence, findings, resolutions, explicit sign-off on test amendments, and verdict.

## Delivery

Candidate identity, combined proof and walkthrough link at `<main-repo>/.agent-docs/proofs/<feature>.md`, and remaining work or cleanup.
